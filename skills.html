Learning Method

My current approach is to understand the underlying behavior before adding frameworks, abstractions, or extra tooling.

  • 01
    Study one concept

    Understand the purpose before copying an implementation.

  • 02
    Build a small example

  • 03
    Debug deliberately

    Read errors and test assumptions instead of guessing.

  • 04
    Record the result

    Commit completed steps and keep concise technical notes.
